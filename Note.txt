  Add a safety buffer to the self-driving car so it never crosses into or hits detected objects. But if the only thing it detects is a pedestrian, it should decide on its own to move as close to the pedestrian as safely possible. 
  That means, in that case, it should change the buffer range to the minimum. This is necessary because if there is a gap between the vehicle and the pedestrian, a cyclist could enter the space, proceed ahead, and block the vehicle’s path.
